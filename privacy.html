🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

At LiquorBelle, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

📌 Quick Summary: We collect only the information needed to process your orders and deliver your purchases. We never sell your data to third parties. Your payment information is handled securely by Safaricom M-PESA.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address
  • Payment Information: M-PESA transaction details (we never store your M-PESA PIN)
  • Order History: Products purchased, delivery preferences, and order status
  • Communication: Messages you send to us via WhatsApp, email, or phone

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, and interaction with our platform
  • Location Data: Approximate location for delivery zone verification

1.3 Google Authentication Data

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address
  • We do not access your Google contacts, calendar, or other personal data

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Order Processing: To process and deliver your orders
  • Payment Verification: To confirm M-PESA payment transactions
  • Account Management: To maintain your account and preferences
  • Communication: To send order confirmations, delivery updates, and support responses
  • Service Improvement: To analyze usage and improve our platform
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

3. How We Protect Your Information

  • Data Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS
  • Secure Storage: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text
  • JWT Authentication: Secure JSON Web Tokens protect your session
  • Rate Limiting: Protection against brute force attacks
  • Access Control: Role-based access for administrators and staff

🛡️ Security Note: We use industry-standard security practices including bcrypt password hashing, JWT authentication, and HTTPS encryption. Your M-PESA payments are processed directly by Safaricom and we never store your M-PESA PIN.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our platform:

  • Safaricom M-PESA: Payment processing (your payment details are handled by Safaricom)
  • Brevo (Sendinblue): Transactional email delivery (order confirmations, OTPs)
  • MongoDB Atlas: Cloud database hosting
  • Render.com: Backend hosting
  • GitHub Pages: Frontend hosting
  • Cloudinary: Image storage and optimization
  • Google (OAuth): Authentication services

Each of these services has their own privacy policies which we recommend you review.

5. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained as long as your account is active
  • Order History: Retained for up to 7 years for tax and legal purposes
  • Payment Records: Retained for transaction verification and dispute resolution
  • Deleted Accounts: Data is soft-deleted (marked as inactive) for 30 days before permanent deletion

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

7. Age Verification

LiquorBelle is an alcohol delivery service. By using our platform, you confirm that you are 18 years or older. We reserve the right to verify your age through government-issued identification. We do not store copies of your identification documents.

8. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to:

  • Maintain your session and login state
  • Remember your cart and wishlist items
  • Analyze site traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve your user experience

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are strictly for adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we discover we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the new policy on our website
  • Sending an email notification to registered users
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
